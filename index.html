01

Introduction Background and rationale of the study

Why this matters

Freshwater ecosystems — especially rivers — are vital for biodiversity, human health, and local communities. Pollution, urbanization, and improper waste disposal, however, contribute to declining water quality in many areas, including communities around Calapan City.

Traditional water-quality monitoring often relies on manual sampling and laboratory analysis. While these approaches provide useful measurements, they can be time-consuming, labor-intensive, and unable to deliver continuous updates.

AquaSense proposes a solar-powered IoT-based sensor network that monitors pH, turbidity, and temperature. The concept explores how more frequent readings could help identify changes earlier and support informed environmental monitoring.

02

Methodology Research design, locale, and proposed prototype

Research Design

This study utilizes a Quantitative Research Design with a descriptive component. It focuses on gathering numerical data through the AquaSense sensor network to determine the current water quality parameters (pH, turbidity, temperature) of the freshwater ecosystem.

The design is subject to the final approved methodology of the institution, ensuring that data gathering and analysis strictly follow academic and scientific standards.

Locale of the Study

The proposed deployment and field evaluation will be conducted at:

  • Barangay Ibaba West, Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro.

This freshwater site was chosen for its ecological significance to the local community. Any actual field deployment, water sampling, and physical evaluation should be properly documented and coordinated with local authorities.

Participants and Evaluators

The intended subject-matter evaluators for this prototype may include:

  • Science and IT Teachers
  • Qualified Environmental Reviewers
  • Local Environmental Officers (if applicable)

Their role is to assess the accuracy, functionality, and feasibility of the AquaSense prototype according to the approved study protocol and rubrics of the institution.

Solar Floating Platform

Function Provides buoyancy and structural support, allowing the system to float autonomously on the water surface while housing the electronics in a weather-resistant enclosure.

☀️

Solar Panel & Battery

Function Harvests solar energy to power the ESP32 and sensors. The battery stores excess power for nighttime operation, ensuring 24/7 monitoring.

🧠

ESP32 Controller

Function Acts as the central processing unit. It reads analog signals from the sensors, converts them into digital values, and manages the Wi-Fi connection to Firebase.

🌡️

Sensor Array

Function Submerged probes that continuously measure key water quality parameters (pH, turbidity, and temperature) and feed the analog data to the ESP32.

☁️

Cloud Link (Firebase)

Function Serves as the backend. The ESP32 pushes JSON payloads to Firebase every 3 seconds, which acts as a relay for this web dashboard to read from.

💻

Web Dashboard

Function The front-end interface (this website). It listens for updates from Firebase, visualizes trends using charts, and evaluates data against standard thresholds.

Data Collection and Analysis

To ensure reliable and accurate readings, the following procedures are planned:

  • Sensor validation — comparing AquaSense readings against standard laboratory instruments.
  • Repeatability checks — placing sensors in the same water sample multiple times to check consistency.
  • Power measurements — monitoring battery and solar panel efficiency over time.
  • Network & transmission tests — evaluating Wi-Fi range and Firebase data logging reliability.

Latest sensor readings are transmitted every 3 seconds and stored in the cloud for trend analysis and historical review.

06

Conclusion Findings and future work

AquaSense presents a proposed solar-powered IoT approach to monitoring selected freshwater parameters: pH, turbidity, and temperature. By combining sensors, a microcontroller, wireless communication, and a web dashboard, the concept aims to make readings easier to review and support timely follow-up.

The dashboard displays live data streamed from the ESP32 sensor unit via Firebase Realtime Database. Readings update automatically and are stored for historical review.

❝

AquaSense is a step toward more accessible freshwater monitoring and healthier aquatic ecosystems.
